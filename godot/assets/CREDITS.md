# Credits — Козырная Арена assets

All card frames, portraits backgrounds, UI elements, table textures and the logo are
original procedural art generated by `godot/tools/asset-studio/` (HTML canvas + headless Chrome).
No third-party artwork is included.

## Icons — game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0)
The hero and magic figures in `assets/portraits/` are rendered from icon shapes from
https://game-icons.net, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Icons were recolored, shaded and composited.

Authors: carl-olsen, caro-asercion, cathelineau, darkzaitzev, delapouite, lorc, sbed, skoll, willdabeast
